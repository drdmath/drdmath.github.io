About

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Morningside Center of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in number theory, Diophantine geometry, Arakelov geometry, and homogeneous dynamics. I am also interested in automorphic forms and L-functions.

I am particularly interested in abelian varieties, Shimura varieties, and algebraic groups.

Problems of particular interest to me concern rational points on algebraic varieties, special points on Shimura varieties, and unlikely intersections, including the Mordell–Lang conjecture, the Bogomolov conjecture, the André–Oort conjecture and its generalizations, and the Zilber–Pink conjecture. I am also interested in equidistribution problems in number theory.

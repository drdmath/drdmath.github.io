Zeros of Dirichlet L-functions and One-Sided Estimates

Abstract. We study a one-sided criterion for the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. For each modulus the criterion involves a smoothed count of prime powers in a progression weighted by a quadratic character. It suffices to bound this remainder at squares. A comparison of covolumes of families of automorphic forms would supply the bound. We construct short rows and compute their Gram forms under theta lifts. The signed lower height on the same rows, including its integral indices and local Hecke labels, remains open. Dirichlet GRH is the target conjecture.

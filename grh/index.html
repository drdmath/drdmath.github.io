GRH Human-Readable Proof Project (Ongoing)

The generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions
Ruida Di

Overview

This project aims to develop a complete, rigorous, and human-readable proof of the generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. The current manuscripts represent work in progress and will be gradually revised, refined, and updated as the project develops.

Proof status: incomplete. The current manuscripts do not constitute a verified proof of GRH. Volume I describes the proposed reduction and the estimates that remain to be established. The arguments are subject to further revision and verification.

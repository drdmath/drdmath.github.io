About

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Morningside Center of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My postdoctoral mentor is Professor Ye Tian.

I received my Ph.D. from Leibniz University Hannover in 2024, advised by Professor Ziyang Gao.

Research Interests

My research interests are in number theory, Diophantine geometry, Arakelov geometry, homogeneous dynamics, automorphic forms, and L-functions.

I am particularly interested in abelian varieties, Shimura varieties, and algebraic groups.

I work on problems concerning rational points, unlikely intersections, and equidistribution.
